Deepseek is AGI 🤣

A Firefox extension that right-clicks a YouTube video, pulls the transcript and summarises it. Hermes tested it on Rick Astley.

I had a brilliant idea.

Scroll through YouTube and there are at least a billion videos that run 30 minutes or longer. Many of them look interesting enough, so you click one, have no idea what’s going on, and burn 30 to 40 minutes trying to figure it out. In the end, it may or may not have mattered at all.

So I made something: a Firefox plugin.

All it does is let me right-click a video on YouTube. Behind the scenes:

  1. yt-dlp downloads the transcript.
  2. The transcript is fed straight into OpenCode.
  3. OpenCode sits through the whole thing, figures out what the video is actually about, and gives me a short, quick summary.

How agents build things

When you build something with an agent, it usually runs a build loop. It creates something, tests it, and if it finds bugs, it loops back, fixes them, and tests again. Once it’s finished, it shows you the outcome with a report. That’s more or less how the whole agent thing works.

The test

So which video did Hermes pick to test this program?

“Never Gonna Give You Up” by Rick Astley.

The Chinese DeepSeek AI has achieved AGI-tier intelligence, all for that one simple thing. 🤣🤣🤣
